## Full description (max 4000)

DocNook opens, scans, converts and edits your documents on your phone. Everything happens on the device: no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no internet permission.

READ ANY DOCUMENT
• PDF: smooth scrolling, zoom, page jump, thumbnails, table of contents, search, select and copy text, bookmarks, night mode and password-protected PDFs.
• Word (DOCX, DOTX), Excel (XLSX, XLSM, CSV, TSV) and PowerPoint (PPTX).
• OpenDocument (ODT, ODS), Rich Text (RTF) and a text preview of old DOC, XLS and PPT files.
• E-books (EPUB), Markdown, plain text, logs, JSON, XML and code with syntax colours.
• Images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, HEIC) and ZIP archives: browse and open files without extracting everything.
• Read Aloud: listen to PDFs, Word files, e-books and text with your phone's own voices, with each sentence highlighted as it is read.
• Reopens every document where you left off.

SCAN
• Scan paper documents with automatic edge detection, multi-page batches and perspective correction.
• Filters: Document (black and white), Grayscale, Enhanced colour.
• ID card mode puts the front and back on one page at real size; Book mode splits a two-page spread.
• Text recognition (OCR) makes searchable PDFs and copyable text.
• Save as PDF or JPG in small, balanced or best quality.

CONVERT
• Images to PDF and PDF to JPG or PNG.
• Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text and Markdown to PDF.
• PDF to Word (DOCX) and PDF to text.
• CSV to Excel and Excel to CSV.
• Convert many files at once.
Each conversion shows an honest quality label before you start.

PDF TOOLS
• Merge PDFs and images, split by range, extract pages.
• Organize pages: reorder, rotate, delete, duplicate, insert blank or image pages.
• Compress PDFs in three strengths.
• Protect with an AES-256 password or remove a password you know.
• Watermark, page numbers, sign, annotate (highlight, underline, strike, draw, notes).
• Fill PDF forms and redact: hidden text is truly removed, not just covered.
Your original file is never changed unless you choose to overwrite it.

ORGANIZE
• Finds the documents on your phone automatically, grouped by type.
• Recents, Starred and Collections.
• Search file names and the text inside your documents, including scanned text.
• Rename, duplicate, share, open with another app, and see file details.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
DocNook does not collect any data. Your files stay on your phone and leave it only when you share them.
